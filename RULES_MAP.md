# Pathfinder 2E Rulebook Map

This file provides a detailed overview of where everything is located within this vault. It focuses on the rulebook content and ignores supporting files like images or licenses.

---

## 📖 Core Rulebooks & Mechanics
Located in `rules/rules/`

These folders contain the structural rules of the game, divided by sourcebook or mechanic type.

| Folder/File | Description | Key Content |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| `core-rulebook/` | The fundamental rules of Pathfinder 2E. | Chapters on character creation, playing the game, and GMing basics. |
| `gamemastery-guide/` | Advanced tools for Game Masters. | Subsystems (chases, research), variant rules, and NPC creation. |
| `actions/` | A comprehensive list of actions. | Includes basic actions (Stride, Strike) and specialized ones from various books. |
| `traits/` | The glossary of Pathfinder 2E. | Every trait (e.g., *Agile*, *Fire*, *Mental*) with its mechanical definition. |
| `abilities/` | Universal rules and monster abilities. | Definitions for *Darkvision*, *Attack of Opportunity*, *Grab*, etc. |
| `tables/` | Mechanical reference tables. | DC by level, XP awards, encounter budget tables, etc. |
| `conditions.md` | Single-file reference for conditions. | *Frightened*, *Sickened*, *Stunned*, and all other status effects. |
| `lost-omens-*/` | World-building and setting rules. | Specific rules from *Gods & Magic*, *World Guide*, and *Gods & Magic*. |
| `secrets-of-magic/` | Specialized magic rules. | Rules for Pervasive Magic, Ley Lines, and Soul Seeds. |

## 🛠️ Compendium (The Database)
Located in `rules/compendium/`

The "nuts and bolts" of the system. This is a massive collection of individual files for every item, feat, and spell in the game.

### Character Options
*   `character/archetypes/`: Every multiclass and standalone archetype (e.g., *Beastmaster*, *Fighter Dedication*).
*   `character/backgrounds/`: A huge list of backgrounds from all official sources.
*   `feats/`: All general, skill, and class feats (note: many classes may point here for their specific feat lists).
*   `skills.md`: A summary file for all core skills and their associated actions.

### Magic & Spells
*   `spells/`: A massive directory containing over 1,200 spells and cantrips.
*   `spells/rituals/`: Complex magic rituals (e.g., *Resurrection*, *Ward Ward*).
*   `setting/deities/`: Detailed entries for gods, including their edicts, anathema, and granted powers.
*   `setting/domains.md`: Reference for all divine domains and their associated spells.

### Items & Equipment
*   `equipment/`: Categorized subfolders for weapons, armor, adventuring gear, magic items, and consumables.

### GM Tools & Hazards
*   `gm/afflictions/`: Detailed rules for poisons, diseases, and curses.
*   `gm/hazards/`: Traps and environmental dangers (e.g., *Scything Blade*, *Abyssal Rift*).

## 🐉 Bestiary (Creatures)
Located in `rules/fantasy-bestiary/`

Formatted statblocks for use with the *Fantasy Statblocks* plugin.

*   `bestiary-1 / 2 / 3`: The primary creatures from the three main Bestiary volumes.
*   `gm-guide/`: Specifically formatted NPCs and common humanoid foes.
*   `trouble-in-otari/`: Creatures specific to the *Trouble in Otari* adventure.

---

## 🎨 Styling & Snippets
Located in `rules/Optionals/` and `.obsidian/`

*   `Optionals/action-icons/`: Resources for displaying the 1-2-3 action icons in text.
*   `.obsidian/snippets/pf2-compendium.css`: The primary CSS file that makes the callouts and statblocks look like official Pathfinder books.

## 🔍 Where to find...

*   **How do I play?** Check `rules/rules/core-rulebook/chapter-9-playing-the-game.md`.
*   **What does this word mean?** Look in `rules/rules/traits/` or `rules/rules/conditions.md`.
*   **I need a specific spell!** Search in `rules/compendium/spells/`.
*   **How do I build a monster?** See `rules/rules/gamemastery-guide/chapter-2-tools.md`.
*   **What are the DC requirements?** Look in `rules/rules/tables/`.
