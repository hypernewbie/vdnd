# Pathfinder 2e Character Creation & Advancement

## Chapter 1: Level 1 Character Creation

This chapter outlines the step-by-step process for creating a 1st-level player character.

## Step 1: Create a Concept
- **Source**: [chapter-1-introduction.md](rules/rules/core-rulebook/chapter-1-introduction.md)
- Determine the character's history, personality, and role in the party.
- Choose a general direction for Ancestry, Background, and Class.

## Step 2: Initialize Ability Scores
- **Source**: [chapter-1-introduction.md](rules/rules/core-rulebook/chapter-1-introduction.md)
- All six ability scores (Strength, Dexterity, Constitution, Intelligence, Wisdom, Charisma) start at **10**.

## Step 3: Select Ancestry
- **Source**: [chapter-2-ancestries-backgrounds.md](rules/rules/core-rulebook/chapter-2-ancestries-backgrounds.md)
- **Ancestry Choice**: Pick an ancestry (e.g., Human, Elf, Dwarf).
- **Ability Boosts/Flaws**: Apply ancestry-specific boosts (usually +2) and flaws (usually -2).
- **Hit Points**: Note the starting HP from ancestry.
- **Languages**: Record these based on ancestry. If your Intelligence modifier is +1 or higher, you can select additional languages equal to your Intelligence modifier.
- **Heritage**: Select one heritage (e.g., Rock Dwarf).
- **Ancestry Feat**: Choose one 1st-level ancestry feat.

## Step 4: Select Background
- **Source**: [chapter-2-ancestries-backgrounds.md](rules/rules/core-rulebook/chapter-2-ancestries-backgrounds.md)
- **Background Choice**: Pick a background (e.g., Nomad, Noble).
- **Ability Boosts**: Apply two ability boosts (+2 each). One is usually restricted to two choices; the other is free.
- **Skills**: Become trained in a specific skill and a corresponding Lore skill.
    - *Note: If your class would make you trained in a skill you're already trained in from your background, you can select another skill to become trained in instead.*
- **Skill Feat**: Gain the specific 1st-level skill feat associated with the background.

## Step 5: Select Class
- **Source**: [chapter-3-classes.md](rules/rules/core-rulebook/chapter-3-classes.md)
- **Class Choice**: Pick a class (e.g., Fighter, Wizard).
- **Key Ability Boost**: Apply a +2 boost to the class's key ability score.
- **Initial Proficiencies**: Note proficiency ranks (Untrained, Trained, Expert) for Perception, Saving Throws, Skills, Attacks, and Defenses.
- **Hit Points**: Add class-specific HP.
- **Class Features**: Record 1st-level class features and any starting class feats.

## Step 6: Finalize Ability Scores
- **Source**: [chapter-1-introduction.md](rules/rules/core-rulebook/chapter-1-introduction.md)
- **Free Boosts**: Apply **four additional free boosts** to four *different* ability scores (+2 each).
- **Calculate Modifiers**: For each score, calculate the modifier: `(Score - 10) / 2` (rounded down).
    - *Note: At 1st level, scores cannot exceed 18.*

## Step 7: Record Class Details
- **Sources**: [chapter-1-introduction.md](rules/rules/core-rulebook/chapter-1-introduction.md), [chapter-3-classes.md](rules/rules/core-rulebook/chapter-3-classes.md)
- **Total HP**: `Ancestry HP + Class HP + Constitution Modifier`.
- **Perception**: `Proficiency Bonus + Wisdom Modifier`.
- **Saving Throws**:
    - Fortitude: `Proficiency Bonus + Constitution Modifier`.
    - Reflex: `Proficiency Bonus + Dexterity Modifier`.
    - Will: `Proficiency Bonus + Wisdom Modifier`.
- **Proficiency Bonus**: `Level + Rank Constant` (Trained=2, Expert=4, Master=6, Legendary=8).

## Step 8: Buy Equipment
- **Source**: [chapter-6-equipment.md](rules/rules/core-rulebook/chapter-6-equipment.md)
- **Starting Wealth**: **15 gold pieces (150 sp)**.
- **Armor/Weapons**: Purchase based on proficiencies.
- **Bulk**: Calculate total Bulk carried. Limit: `5 + Strength Modifier` (Encumbered), `10 + Strength Modifier` (Max).

## Step 9: Final Calculations
- **Sources**: [chapter-1-introduction.md](rules/rules/core-rulebook/chapter-1-introduction.md), [chapter-9-playing-the-game.md](rules/rules/core-rulebook/chapter-9-playing-the-game.md)
- **Armor Class (AC)**: `10 + Dexterity Modifier (capped by armor) + Proficiency Bonus + Item Bonus`.
- **Attack Modifiers**:
    - Melee: `Proficiency Bonus + Strength Modifier + Item Bonus`.
    - Ranged: `Proficiency Bonus + Dexterity Modifier + Item Bonus`.
- **Damage**:
    - Melee: `Weapon Die + Strength Modifier`.
    - Ranged: `Weapon Die (+ Strength if Propulsive/Thrown)`.
- **Skill Modifiers**: `Proficiency Bonus + Ability Modifier + Other Bonuses/Penalties`.

## Step 10: Finishing Touches
- **Sources**: [chapter-1-introduction.md](rules/rules/core-rulebook/chapter-1-introduction.md)
- **Alignment**: Choose one of 9 alignments.
- **Deity**: Select a deity (required for Clerics/Champions).
- **Age/Gender/Pronouns**: Record personal details.
- **Class DC**: `10 + Proficiency Bonus + Key Ability Modifier`.
- **Hero Points**: Start with **1 Hero Point**.

## Chapter 2: Leveling Up (Levels 2–20)

Advancement in Pathfinder 2e occurs every time a character reaches **1,000 Experience Points (XP)**.

### Step 1: Incremental Leveling
- **Source**: [chapter-1-introduction.md](rules/rules/core-rulebook/chapter-1-introduction.md)
- **Increase Level**: Increment your character's level by 1.
- **Reset XP**: Subtract 1,000 XP from your total. Carry over any excess XP.

### Step 2: Immediate Vitality
- **Source**: [chapter-1-introduction.md](rules/rules/core-rulebook/chapter-1-introduction.md)
- **Increase Hit Points**: Add the number of HP granted by your class to your maximum.
- **Retroactive HP**: If an Ability Boost increases your Constitution modifier, add 1 HP per level retrospectively (e.g., at level 5, adding 5 HP).

### Step 3: Class Advancement
- **Sources**: [chapter-3-classes.md](rules/rules/core-rulebook/chapter-3-classes.md), [chapter-4-skills.md](rules/rules/core-rulebook/chapter-4-skills.md)
- **Feature Gains**: Consult your class's advancement table for features gained at the new level.
- **Feats**: Select new feats as specified (Class, Skill, General).
- **Skill Increases**: Apply skill increases as granted by your class.
- **Spellcasting**: Add new spell slots and select new spells if your class is a spellcasting class.

### Step 4: Ancestry Advancement
- **Source**: [chapter-2-ancestries-backgrounds.md](rules/rules/core-rulebook/chapter-2-ancestries-backgrounds.md)
- **Ancestry Feats**: Gain a new ancestry feat at levels **5, 9, 13, and 17**.

### Step 5: Ability Score Boosts
- **Source**: [chapter-1-introduction.md](rules/rules/core-rulebook/chapter-1-introduction.md)
- **Frequency**: Every 5 levels (**5, 10, 15, 20**).
- **Application**: Apply **four free boosts** to four *different* ability scores.
    - If a score is < 18: Boost increases it by 2.
    - If a score is >= 18: Boost increases it by 1.
- **Intelligence Impacts**: If Intelligence increases, select a new trained skill and a new language.

### Step 6: Recalculate Modifiers
- **Source**: [chapter-9-playing-the-game.md](rules/rules/core-rulebook/chapter-9-playing-the-game.md)
- **Level Bonus**: Increase all proficiency-based modifiers by **1**. 
    - *Includes: Perception, Saving Throws, Attacks, AC, Skill Modifiers, Spell Attack, Spell DC, and Class DC.*
- **Modifier Changes**: Re-evaluate any statistics affected by new Ability Boosts or improved Proficiency Ranks (e.g., moving from Trained to Expert).
- **Gear Check**: Ensure magic items or upgraded equipment (item bonuses) are applied.

## Chapter 3: Classes

Pathfinder 2e offers a wide variety of classes across multiple sourcebooks. Each class defines a character's role, key ability scores, and progression.

### Core Rulebook Classes
**Source**: [chapter-3-classes.md](rules/rules/core-rulebook/chapter-3-classes.md)
The 12 base classes available in the basic game:
- **Alchemist**: Resourceful crafter of bombs, elixirs, and poisons.
- **Barbarian**: Fearsome warrior who channels rage into combat power.
- **Bard**: Performer who bolsters allies with occult magic and music.
- **Champion**: Devout defender of a deity's tenets and protector of allies.
- **Cleric**: Worshiper who draws divine magic from their deity.
- **Druid**: Nature devotee who commands primal magic and wild shapes.
- **Fighter**: Unmatched master of weaponry and battlefield tactics.
- **Monk**: Unarmed combatant who seeks physical and mental perfection.
- **Ranger**: Expert tracker and hunter who excels in the wild.
- **Rogue**: Cunning master of skills and precision attacks.
- **Sorcerer**: Innate spellcaster whose magic comes from their bloodline.
- **Wizard**: Scholarly master of arcane formulas and spells.

### Secrets of Magic Classes
**Source**: [chapter-2-classes.md](rules/rules/secrets-of-magic/chapter-2-classes.md)
- **Magus**: Spellcaster-warrior who channels magic through weapon strikes.
- **Summoner**: Conjurer bonded to a powerful extraplanar eidolon.

### Advanced Player's Guide Classes
**Source**: [Advanced Player's Guide Traits](rules/rules/traits/)
- **Investigator**: Master of deduction who devises stratagems in combat.
- **Oracle**: Conduit of divine power burdened by a mysterious curse.
- **Swashbuckler**: Showy combatant who performs stunts to gain panache.
- **Vigilante**: Hero with a dual identity, balancing social and combat personas.
- **Witch**: Spellcaster who gains magic from a mysterious patron via a familiar.

### Guns & Gears Classes
**Source**: [Guns & Gears Traits](rules/rules/traits/)
- **Gunslinger**: Master of firearms and crossbows.
- **Inventor**: Brilliant engineer who fights with a customized innovation.

### Dark Archive Classes
**Source**: [Dark Archive Traits](rules/rules/traits/)
- **Psychic**: Mental spellcaster who channels power through their mind.
- **Thaumaturge**: Occult expert who uses esoterica and implements to exploit weaknesses.

### Rage of Elements Classes
**Source**: [Kineticist Trait](rules/rules/traits/kineticist-roe.md)
- **Kineticist**: Elemental conduit who shapes energy from the Inner Spheres.
